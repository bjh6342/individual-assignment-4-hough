ABOUT ME

Who am I?

I'm Benjamin Hough, a Graphic Design and/or Digital Multimedia Design student. I originally studied Graphic Design at Pittsburgh Technical College prior to their closure in 2024, at which point I tranferred to studying Digital Multimedia Design at Penn State World Campus.

I originally became interested in Graphic Design due to my love of Digital Art at a very young age. Since beginning to hone my skills at PTC, I have become more proficient in photo editing, digital art, typography, 3D modeling, web design, and much more.
