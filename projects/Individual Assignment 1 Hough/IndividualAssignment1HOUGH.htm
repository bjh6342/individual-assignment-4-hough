Help Me Pay My Tuition: Selling My Pokémon Cards

About My Collection

One of the hardest decisions I have ever made is selling my collection of Pokémon cards to help pay my college tuition bill. I began collecting cards when I was a child and have spent years building the collection.

The collection includes several classic cards from the 90s as well as newer releases. Some of my favorite cards are the Special Edition Topps Brand cards. These cards have been carefully stored and preserved. It is bittersweet to part with them because they hold many childhood memories.

Although I hate to see them go, my education must come first. I hope these cards will find a new home with someone who appreciates Pokémon as much as I do.

Why My Collection Is Special?

  • Was passed down to me from my brothers
  • Contains special edition Topps cards
  • Cards have been carefully preserved
  • Built over many years of collecting, including cards from the 90s to present day.

Rare Items for Sale

  1. Special Edition Topps Cards, including Venusaur, Charizard and Arcanine.
  2. Many classic 90s cards
  3. Artbox Action Flipz Cards
  4. Many EX and Fullart cards
