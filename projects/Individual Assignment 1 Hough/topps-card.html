Special Edition Topps Charizard Card

This Special Edition Topps Charizard card is one of my favorite items in the collection. It has been stored in a protective sleeve and remains in excellent condition. I believe it is the rarest among my Topps collection.

The card features the final form of the first ever fire type Pokémon. It's a great collectible, especially for those who like more "niche" finds.
