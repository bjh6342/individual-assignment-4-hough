Trad Climbing: What is it

Learn about the traditional style of climbing.

Trad

Figure 1.1: Trad Climber.

Trad climbing is when you place your anchors in the rock while you are climbing. Normal climbing already has preplaced anchors for you. These preplaced ones limit where you can go on the wall and what rocks you can climb. However, with trad climbing you can Climb Where Ever You Want.


Trad climbing requires special equipment and takes a long time to learn. Current climbing gyms do not have walls for people to practice there trad climbing. This means that to learn how to trad climb you have to do it outdoors where it is much more dangerous. With the new Great Rocks climbing gyms can now install walls for people to learn trad climbing in a save envorment.


With so few gyms having this feature you can bring in more people to your gym that would go somewhere else.
